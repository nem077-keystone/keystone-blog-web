# Hot vs. Cold Wallets: Understanding the Basics
Cryptocurrency wallets generally fall into two categories: hot wallets and cold wallets. Hot wallets are always connected to the internet, providing quick access to your funds but at the cost of higher vulnerability to cyber threats. Cold wallets, on the other hand, store your digital assets offline, creating a secure environment that is much harder for hackers to penetrate.

## The Mechanics of Keystone Cold Wallets
At the heart of Keystone's cold wallet technology is the concept of air-gapped security. Keystone 3 Pro operates completely offline, generating and storing private keys in a secure environment that is isolated from the internet. This physical isolation is enhanced by the use of QR codes and Micro SD cards for data transfer, ensuring that your keys are never exposed online.

Furthermore, Keystone's cold wallets are embedded with three distinct secure elements—each playing a unique role in protecting your recovery phrases and biometric data. This multi-layered approach makes it nearly impossible for unauthorized parties to access your funds.
