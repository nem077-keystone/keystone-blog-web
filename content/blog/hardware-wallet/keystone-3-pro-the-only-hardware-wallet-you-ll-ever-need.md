# Air-Gapped Security That Actually Works

While most wallets rely on potentially vulnerable wireless connections, the Keystone 3 Pro takes a different approach. It operates **completely offline by default, using QR codes and microSD cards for all communication. No Bluetooth. No Wi-Fi**. This air-gapped architecture creates an impenetrable barrier that keeps hackers at bay.

For users who need wired connectivity, the device thoughtfully includes a USB port for specific tasks like firmware updates and MetaMask integration — **all while maintaining its security-first design philosophy.**

# Use Your Fingerprint 

Nobody enjoys typing complex passwords repeatedly. The Keystone 3 Pro eliminates this friction with optional fingerprint authentication, ensuring that only you can unlock your device. Even if someone gains physical access to your wallet, your biometric data keeps your assets protected.

# Triple Security Chips – An Industry First

While most hardware wallets rely on a single security chip, Keystone 3 Pro employs three specialized processors:

- **Recovery Phrase Protection Chip** — Safeguards your seed words with dedicated encryption
- **Secure Element Chip** — Acts as your digital vault's impenetrable door
- **Fingerprint Security Chip** — Securely stores and validates your biometric data

This triple-layer approach doesn't just improve security — it redefines what's possible in hardware wallet protection.
