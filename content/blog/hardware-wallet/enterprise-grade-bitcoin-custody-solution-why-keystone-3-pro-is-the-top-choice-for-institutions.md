The Keystone 3 Pro, a **bitcoin cold storage wallet** and **secure crypto wallet**, is specifically designed to meet institutional needs, excelling in security, asset segregation, and offline firmware upgrades.


## Meeting Institutional Security Standards

Security certification is a critical factor for institutions when selecting a wallet solution. The Keystone 3 Pro is **EAL6+ certified**, surpassing industry security standards to ensure top-tier protection. Unlike competing products that rely on **a single security chip**, Keystone 3 Pro features **three independent security chips**, providing superior defense against both physical and software-based attacks, significantly reducing the risk of asset compromise.


## Asset Segregation with Multi-Seed Management

For institutions managing large investment portfolios, asset segregation is crucial. The Keystone 3 Pro **supports up to 3 separate seed phrases**, allowing organizations to manage multiple independent wallets within the same device. This ensures complete isolation between different accounts, enhancing security while optimizing multi-account management. In contrast, competitor wallets that **only support a single seed phrase** present a **single point of failure**, putting all assets at risk if compromised.



## Enhanced Security with Offline Firmware Updates

Firmware updates play a vital role in maintaining wallet security. The Keystone 3 Pro **uses MicroSD card-based offline updates**, eliminating risks associated with USB connections. Competitor wallets that rely solely on USB updates expose themselves to potential malware and cyber threats during the upgrade process. By contrast, Keystone 3 Pro ensures a **completely air-gapped update mechanism**, safeguarding the firmware from external vulnerabilities.
