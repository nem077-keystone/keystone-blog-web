### The Drawbacks of Traditional Hardware Wallets

Traditional hardware wallets, like those featuring small monochrome screens and two physical buttons, pose several challenges:
![The Drawbacks of Traditional Hardware Wallets.jpg](/images/blog/hardware-wallet/how-to-choose-the-best-hardware-wallet-in-2024-a-comprehensive-guide/the-drawbacks-of-traditional-hardware-wallets-49329fab5c.jpg)

1. **Limited Usability**: Navigating through a tiny screen and physical buttons can be cumbersome and error-prone, especially for complex transactions.
2. **Risk of Blind Signing**: With small screens, users may not fully see or understand the details of their transactions, increasing the risk of approving malicious transactions.
3. **Lack of Advanced Security Features**: Traditional wallets often lack advanced security measures such as biometric authentication and multiple secure elements.
