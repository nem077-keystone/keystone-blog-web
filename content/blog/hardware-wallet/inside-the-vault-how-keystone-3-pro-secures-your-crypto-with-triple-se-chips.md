# What Are Secure Elements (SEs), and Why Should You Care?

Secure Elements are super secure chips built to **resist both digital hacks and physical tampering**. You’ll find them in bank cards, passports, and now… in the Keystone 3 Pro.

Here’s what they protect you from:

- Hackers trying to read your chip’s power usage (called power analysis attacks)
- Cold boot attacks (where hackers try to extract info after the device is shut down)
- Fault attacks (tricking the device into making errors)
- Physical tampering (like someone trying to open the device)

These chips are independently tested by third-party labs, so **the security isn’t just marketing — it’s verified.**

# How Keystone 3 Pro Takes It to the Next Level

The Keystone 3 Pro features three powerful Secure Elements, and each has a specific job:

- **Microchip ATECC608B** – Secures your recovery phrase
- **Maxim DS28S60** – Creates a trusted environment for recovery storage
- **Maxim MAX32520** – Secures your fingerprint (only on the Pro model)

These chips work together to lock down your most sensitive data, making Keystone 3 Pro nearly impossible to crack.

# Tamper-Proof, Hacker-Resistant

The Keystone 3 Pro isn’t just secure, **it’s tamper-reactive**.

If someone tries to physically open your device, a special “security house” inside the chip will automatically wipe all your data. That’s a PCI-grade feature, meaning it’s the kind of protection banks use.

# Your Seed Phrase Is Locked Down: Here’s How

Keystone doesn’t just encrypt your seed with your password, it also uses two SE chips to create a unique encryption key behind the scenes.

This means that even if someone gets your password, they still can’t access your seed phrase without compromising both secure chips, which is virtually impossible.
