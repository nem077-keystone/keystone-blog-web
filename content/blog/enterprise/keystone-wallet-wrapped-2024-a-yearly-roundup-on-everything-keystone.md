Reflecting on 2024, it’s impossible to see it as anything but a game-changer for Keystone. This was a year of bold innovation, meaningful growth, and stronger connections with our community. 

We pushed boundaries to elevate your self-custody experience, tackled challenges head-on, and introduced solutions that truly matter. Above all, we remained steadfast in our mission: empowering you to own your crypto journey with confidence.

Let’s take a moment to reflect on the milestones and achievements that defined Keystone’s 2024 journey.


## 1. In 2024, we expanded our chain support with powerful integrations

2024 was undoubtedly the year of integration. We’ve worked tirelessly to make Keystone a versatile and powerful tool by expanding support to 12 new blockchains and 18 software wallets. Here’s a closer look at what we accomplished:

**Bitcoin**: Zeus Wallet.
**Ordinals** & Runes: UniSat Wallet.
**Arweave and AO**: ArConnect.
**Bitcoin Cash**: OKX Wallet.
**Cardano**: Begin Wallet, Typhon Wallet, Vespr Wallet, and Medusa Wallet.
**Cosmos**: Mintscan.
**Dash**: OKX Wallet.
**EVM**: Bitget Wallet.
**Helium**: Helium Wallet.
**Litecoin**: OKX Wallet.
**Solana**: Backpack Wallet.
**Stellar**: XBull Wallet.
**Sui**: Suiet Wallet and Nightly Wallet.
**ThorChain**: ThorWallet.
**Tron**: OKX Wallet.
**Ton**: Tonkeeper.
**Zcash**: Zashi Wallet.

Read more in: https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/1811388784229114011

Each integration reflects our commitment to keeping Keystone at the forefront of blockchain technology, ensuring you’re equipped to navigate an ever-expanding ecosystem with confidence.

## 2. Not just integrations, we also added some exciting features:

We didn’t stop at integrations. Our team has been hard at work adding features designed to make Web3 operations more secure and user-friendly. Here are some highlights from this year:

#### **Keystone-Specific Features:**
- Dice Roll Entropy: Improved randomness for secure key generation.
- NFT Lock Screen Support: Customize your device with your favorite NFTs.
- Permit Signing Management & Warning: Giving you control over offline signature.
#### **BoldBitcoin Ecosystem**:
- Support for Taproot, ARC20, BRC20, Ordinals, and Runes.
- Focused BTC-Only Firmware for dedicated Bitcoin users.
- Message Signing on Sparrow Wallet and USB Signing with UniSat Wallet.
#### **EVM:**
- ERC20 Token Transaction Analysis for better transparency.
- USB Signing with Rabby Wallet.
- Safe Multi-Sig Contract Parsing to streamline complex transactions.
#### **Cardano:**
- Added support for Catalyst, Conway, and signData.
- Integration of Ledger/Bitbox Algorithms and Begin ID Support.
#### **Cosmos:**
- Multiple Address Support and Custom Derivation Paths.
- USB Signing with Keplr Wallet.
#### **Solana:**
- Squad Multi-Sig Support and Transaction Data Parsing.
- USB Signing with Backpack Wallet.

Read more in: https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/1775199034912985392

These updates reaffirm our focus on combining security and usability, making Keystone a reliable partner for your crypto journey.
