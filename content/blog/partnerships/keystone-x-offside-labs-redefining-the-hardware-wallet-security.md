### Keystone and Offside Labs: A Partnership of Excellence

Recently, Offside Labs conducted a thorough review of Keystone 3 Pro and detected a hidden vulnerability in our firmware. Thanks for their timely intervention, which helped us to identify and effectively neutralise it in our V-1. 2. 8 firmware update. We encourage all our users to upgrade to the latest firmware for enhanced protection.

Recognising their exceptional work, we quickly offered a bounty and extended our heartfelt gratitude. Impressed by their meticulous approach and commitment to perfection, we knew we had to deepen our collaboration with Offside Labs. This partnership clearly reflects our unwavering commitment to security and is a testament to the power of collective effort within the community.

### A Secure Future with Keystone and Offside Labs

We are confident that the expertise and experience of Offside Labs will significantly bolster the security of Keystone, ensuring the utmost protection of your digital assets. Our collaboration symbolises a shared vision of uncompromised security and trust.

In our continued pursuit of transparency and safety, we’ve always stood by the web3 ethos and embraced open-source by publishing everything from the [Secure Element firmware to the bill of material of hardware components](https://github.com/KeystoneHQ/keystone3-firmware). We have also open-sourced the audit reports from [SlowMist](https://github.com/slowmist/Knowledge-Base/blob/master/open-report-V2/blockchain-application/SlowMist%20Audit%20Report%20-%20Keystone3_en-us.pdf) and [Keylabs](https://github.com/keylabsio/audits) to provide complete transparency on our efforts to make a secure product for our users.

By open-sourcing our product, we enable security experts like Offside Labs to assist us more effectively in identifying, rectifying, and enhancing our systems. This helps us leverage the collaborative efforts of the best minds in the industry to fine-tune our product and provide unmatched security advantages to our deserving users. With your unwavering support, our journey towards unrivalled digital asset security continues stronger than ever!

### About Keystone

[Keystone](https://keyst.one/) is an [open-source](https://blog.keyst.one/the-role-of-open-source-tech-in-keystones-development-4dd77faea752) airgap hardware wallet that utilises an embedded system. Our devices are equipped with three secure element chips to enhance their security further. One of Keystone’s unique features is its ability to support [multiple recovery seed phrases](https://blog.keyst.one/unleashing-the-power-of-multiple-secret-recovery-phrases-with-keystone-3-a-comprehensive-guide-3c3afb997cc4), reducing the need to purchase multiple hardware wallet devices.
