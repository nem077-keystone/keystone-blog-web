# Why mobile app support?

Mobile web traffic has been increasing in the last decade as payments with cryptocurrencies are becoming common in certain parts of the world. Almost [54%](https://www.oberlo.com/statistics/mobile-internet-traffic) of all web traffic was from mobile phones in November 2021. [Data](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2019/02/05/smartphone-ownership-is-growing-rapidly-around-the-world-but-not-always-equally/) shows that just 34% of people on average surveyed within developing countries have access to a desktop, laptop, or tablet while most have internet access via their smartphones. The top 20 ranked mobile apps under the finance category in the North American region usually contain around 3 to 4 cryptocurrency mobile wallets.

Integrating with the MetaMask Mobile Wallet is our first step in expanding Keystone’s usability from desktop to mobile. Our goal is to make Keystone the ultimate choice for all software wallets, regardless of whether it is a browser extension, desktop app, or mobile app. Hopefully, in the future, you’ll also be able to use Keystone to sign into various online services.

# Benefits of QR Signing

There are various benefits of signing with QR codes. The lack of a physical connection due to the inherent nature of QR codes allows users to seamlessly sign transactions without ever needing to expose their hardware wallet to the internet. This allows the hardware wallet to be permanently isolated from any potentially compromised devices, keeping a user’s seed phrase safe.

For those who might be concerned with QR code technology, rest assured that all firmware updates are open-sourced. The Keystone team has even developed a [QR Code verifier](https://github.com/KeystoneHQ/KeystoneQRVerifier), for those who are interested in understanding how a QR code works behind the scenes! Users can always check what data is being transferred via the QR codes if ever required.

The seamless user experience of generating and scanning QR codes is not to be underestimated as well. Many users have started to realize how smooth the signing experience is with QR codes compared to multiple button smashing. The simplicity of QR code scanning has also made many users realize how quick signing can be without worrying about any connectivity issues.

# What’s Next?

Integration with both the [MetaMask Extension Wallet](https://twitter.com/MetaMask/status/1469351219290382341) and MetaMask Mobile Wallet app marks the biggest milestones in Keystone’s Web3 exploration journey. We are very proud to work efficiently and closely with the MetaMask team to deliver these integrations as a joint project. Together with the MetaMask team, we’ve co-authored [EIP-4527](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4527) so both hardware and software wallets would have a standardized method to incorporate QR codes. We are more than pleased to help other Ethereum wallets adopt this QR code model. Feel free to reach out to us via [Twitter](https://twitter.com/KeystoneWallet) for assistance.

Developers who are already running a dApp can help protect their users from blind signing by sharing the ABI of their dApp with our [Smart-Contract Metadata Registry](https://github.com/KeystoneHQ/Smart-Contract-Metadata-Registry). Doing so will allow Keystone users to download the official ABI of your dApp onto a microSD card, which is then slotted into their Keystone hardware device. From then, every transaction displayed will be properly identified on the 4” touch screen. This allows users to verify whether they are interacting with an authentic smart contract or if they are at risk of being potentially phished. Not only would your users feel safer when interacting with your smart contracts, but it would also prevent any impersonators from phishing your user’s funds away.

We truly believe in the future where multiple blockchains will co-exist so we are currently exploring other non-EVM Web3 chains like Solana, Avalanche (X & P-Chain), Terra, Cosmos ecosystem, and NEAR protocol.

# About Keystone

[Keystone](https://keyst.one/) is a 100% air-gapped QR code hardware wallet. The wallet is [designed](/keystone-product-design-principles-cd833bc11125) to maximize attack cost, minimize trust, prevent potential human error, avoid single-point-of-failures and boost interoperability. It was [officially integrated with MetaMask](https://twitter.com/MetaMask/status/1469351219290382341) in December 2021.
