## Essential Tools for Trading, Research & Compliance

### Market Data Aggregators

- **CoinGecko** and **CoinMarketCap**: Track real-time prices, trading volume, and market capitalization for 18 000+ cryptocurrencies across 1 000+ exchanges. 
- **Glassnode** and **Messari**: Provide on-chain analytics (exchange inflows, active addresses) for data-driven strategies.

### Charting & Visualization

- **TradingView**: Industry-standard platform offering customizable charts, multi-indicator strategies, and backtesting for BTC, ETH, and altcoins. 
- **Community Features**: Share analyses and collaborate on market forecasts with other traders.

### Crypto News Platforms

- **CoinDesk** and **Cointelegraph**: Up-to-the-minute coverage on price movements, regulation, and protocol upgrades. 
- **Podcasts & Newsletters**: “What Bitcoin Did” and The Held Report for expert insights and in-depth interviews.

### Blockchain Explorers

- **Etherscan**: Verify transactions, inspect smart contracts, and monitor wallet balances on Ethereum. 
- **Solana Explorer**: Detailed on-chain data for the Solana network.

### Crypto Tax Solutions

- **ZenLedger** and **CoinTracker**: Consolidate transactions across exchanges and DeFi platforms, generate IRS-compliant forms (Schedule D, Form 8949), and streamline reporting workflows.
