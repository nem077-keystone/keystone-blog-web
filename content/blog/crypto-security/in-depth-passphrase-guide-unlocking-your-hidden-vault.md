In the realm of cryptocurrency security, there is a golden rule: "The seed phrase is everything." Most users think they are perfectly safe once they have backed up their 12 or 24 words. However, if you are pursuing a higher level of security protection, or want an extra "lifesaver" in extreme situations, you need to understand the **Passphrase (Hidden Wallet)**.

Today, we will dive deep into this advanced security feature and provide a step-by-step guide on how to operate it accurately on the Keystone hardware wallet.

---

## What is a Passphrase?

### 1. It is an "extension" of the seed phrase, not just a simple password

Many people confuse the Passphrase with the wallet's "startup PIN code." In fact, they are fundamentally different:

* **PIN Code:** This is merely an "access card" to enter the device and does not affect the generation of private keys.
* **Passphrase:** Often referred to as the "25th word." It directly participates in the mathematical calculation of your private keys.

In short: **One set of seed phrases + a different Passphrase = a completely different wallet (and addresses)**. If you enter a different passphrase, the device will calculate a brand new set of public and private keys.

![Passphrase.jpg](/images/blog/crypto-security/in-depth-passphrase-guide-unlocking-your-hidden-vault/passphrase-41b2415119.jpg)

### 2. Why use it?

* **Defense against the "$5 Wrench Attack":** If someone forces you to open your wallet, you can provide a "decoy wallet" without a passphrase (containing a small amount of assets), while hiding your significant assets in a "hidden wallet" protected by a Passphrase.
* **The last line of defense if your seed phrase is compromised:** Even if hackers steal your seed phrase, as long as they don't know your Passphrase, they still cannot access the assets in your hidden wallet.
* **Multi-identity management:** You can set different passphrases for "long-term holding" and "daily trading," achieving physical isolation of assets on the same set of seed phrases.

> **⚠️ Core Warning:** Keystone does not store your passphrase. If you forget it, your assets will be unrecoverable forever. Please make sure to store your Passphrase and seed phrase **separately**.

## Keystone 3 Pro Passphrase Feature Guide

Thanks to the Keystone 3 Pro's 4-inch large touch screen, the experience of entering a passphrase is very smooth. Please follow the official standard steps below:

### 1. How to create or enter a passphrase wallet

1. **Enter Settings:** Unlock the device and tap the **"..." icon** in the upper right corner of the main interface.
2. **Find the Passphrase option:** Go to **"Device Settings"** -> **"Wallet Settings"** -> **"Passphrase"**.
3. **Verify Identity:** Enter your PIN code or use your fingerprint to unlock this feature.
4. **Enter and Confirm:** Use the keyboard to enter the Passphrase you want to set, tap **"Next"**, and enter it again to confirm.
5. **View the new wallet:** After confirming, you will notice that the **MasterFingerPrint (MFP)** on the homepage has changed. This means you have successfully switched to the brand new wallet generated by this passphrase.

> **???? Tip:** At this point, you can scan the QR code to import this "passphrase wallet" into watch-only apps like **OKX Wallet, BlueWallet, or MetaMask** as usual, enabling real-time asset monitoring and convenient trading.

### 2. Advanced Feature: Quick Access

If you need to frequently switch between your main wallet and passphrase wallet, there is no need to repeatedly enter the settings:

* On the device's **PIN code lock screen**, tap **"Passphrase Quick Access"** at the bottom.
* Enter your passphrase directly here for one-click access to your hidden vault.

### 3. How to exit the passphrase wallet (Return to the original wallet)

When you have finished your operations and want to return to the original wallet without a passphrase, there are two ways:

* **Method A (Via Settings):** Repeat the steps for "entering a passphrase". On the passphrase entry screen, **do not enter any characters**, just directly tap **"Next"** and **"Confirm"**.
* **Method B (Via Restart):** For security reasons, the Keystone 3 Pro does not store the passphrase inside the device. You simply need to **restart the device**, and the system will automatically default back to the original wallet.

The Passphrase is the ultimate weapon for advanced protection of crypto assets. It elevates your security level from "physical anti-theft" to the height of "logical isolation."
