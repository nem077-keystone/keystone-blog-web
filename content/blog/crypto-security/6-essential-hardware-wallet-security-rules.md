A hardware wallet’s security depends not only on its robust design but also on the user’s strict adherence to best practices. Below are **six non-negotiable principles**—backed by industry standards—to keep your private keys and digital assets safe. Whether you use Keystone 3 Pro or another hardware wallet, these guidelines ensure airtight protection.



## 1. Never Share Your 24-Word Recovery Phrase  
Your recovery phrase (seed phrase) is the master key to your wallet. **Never** reveal it to anyone—no customer-support agent, no friend, no service—even if they claim it’s for “verification.” Once disclosed, an attacker gains full control of your funds.



## 2. Never Store Your Recovery Phrase Digitally  
Computers, smartphones, and cloud services are prime targets for malware and hackers. **Do not** take photos, type your phrase into a text file, or save it in a password manager. Digital copies can be stolen without your knowledge.



## 3. Securely Store Your Written Copy  
Write your phrase on **durable**, fire- and water-resistant media (e.g., stainless-steel plates) and lock it in a safe or safety-deposit box. For added redundancy, split the phrase into multiple shares and store them in separate, geographically distinct locations.

## 4. Trust Only Your Wallet’s On-Screen Display  
Malware can alter addresses shown on your computer or phone screen. When requesting a receive address, **always verify** it on your hardware wallet’s secure display:

1. In your software wallet, request a receive address.  
2. Connect and open the corresponding app on your hardware wallet.  
3. Your device’s secure screen will display the address—confirm it matches what your software wallet shows before sharing it.



## 5. Treat All Online Interfaces as Potentially Compromised  
Assume any software—wallet apps, web browsers, or email clients—could be infected.  
- **Avoid** copy-pasting addresses from your clipboard (malware can hijack clipboard contents).  
- **Use** QR codes or manual entry instead.  
- **Keep** your devices and wallet firmware up to date to patch vulnerabilities.



## 6. Rely on PCI-Level Anti-Tamper Protection  
High-end hardware wallets like Keystone 3 Pro include **PCI-grade anti-tamper features**:  
- A “security house” of tamper-sensing circuitry around the core IC and secure elements.  
- If the device is opened or physically tampered with, it **automatically wipes internal data**, ensuring your crypto assets remain secure even under attack.
